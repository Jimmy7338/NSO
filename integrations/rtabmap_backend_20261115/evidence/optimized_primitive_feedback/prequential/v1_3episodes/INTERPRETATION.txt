Scope: the implementation's "radar" actual target is newly known occupancy
after combined RGB-D/scan fusion, intersected with its frozen radar proposal.
This is intent-realization yield under the current perception/control stack,
not isolated lidar accuracy. Of S26's 821 newly known cell events, only 67 are
inside predicted masks and 754 (91.84%) are outside; these are descriptive cell
events, not independently referenced coverage area. Conditional yield accuracy
therefore does not establish accurate total gain prediction or exploration
efficiency. All camera prediction masks are empty; its zero in-mask count error
is vacuous, and no camera calibration benefit is established.

The .5 baseline is the preserved gain probability with feedback disabled,
evaluated retrospectively on identical actual frozen events. No physical
no-feedback-policy trajectory or counterfactual motion is fabricated. The
analysis supports a component-level predictive-loss case study; it does not
establish semantic benefit, G/S efficiency superiority, full exploration
completion, independent reconstruction accuracy, or generalization. No
independence-based error bars or significance tests are used.
