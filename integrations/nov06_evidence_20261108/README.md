The physical-entry-scale and clearance-matched variants, including their 2×2 counterexamples, were not deployed.

The actual S40 trial ended `execution_interrupted` after 29 arrivals, with no completed return and CAN zero after close. The bounded fault rings are included for diagnosis, not as a full history. A zero-only passive 90 s probe recorded 4,384 zero and zero nonzero commands, with no tick fault.
