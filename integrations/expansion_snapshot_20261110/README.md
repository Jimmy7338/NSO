# Expansion, SDK depth diagnostics and approved native snapshot repair

The expansion encountered 13 depth-derived occupied cells in a region the user identified as empty. Of 120 associated raw points, 118 lay beyond the declared 4 m depth limit. Later raw free-space evidence contradicted 11 of the 13 cells; two remained unknown. These observations preserve the failure and the residual uncertainty.

The original batched SDK reconfiguration A/B probe was invalid: combining driver levels 1 and 2 produced the incorrect level 3. The invalidation and corrected probe receipts are retained separately. With valid updates, SDK 50/50 left about 0.2% valid depth. SDK 50/100 retained about 56%, compared with about 58% at baseline. The physical SDK50 trial faulted at paid action 6 after 0.401 m, with worker code 3 and supervisor code 2. These source-parameter trials do not establish a remedy for intermittent depth artifacts.

The user approved the native snapshot repair, including its clock and lock stop semantics. The candidate source, tests, patch, review and deployment receipts record that behavior change. The subsequent short12 validation returned after 12 paid actions, with 13 accepted observations and 12/12 verified ROS arrivals, 0.9068 m travel and 146.19 s elapsed. Native fault 8 appeared 17.074333 ms after shutdown began; no pre-shutdown native lock fault was observed. Native Q and semantic negative findings remain in the original reductions; this engineering validation does not establish research-method efficacy.

The main40 validation completed 40 paid actions and 41 accepted observations, with all 40 ROS arrivals verified (34 forward, five left, one right). It travelled 6.7568748267 m over 440.470694 s, returned to its anchor and recorded zero motion afterward. All three nonempty plans had equal G/S scores and zero apertures. Native fault 8 first appeared 36.668216 ms after stop_request_begin; no pre-shutdown native fault was observed. The short12 and main40 runs used the approved native snapshot profile. These dependent physical validations do not establish general loaded stability or a causal SDK50/100 repair.
