# Publication evidence scope

This package retains the frozen Q1 simulation data, the actual calibrated C/S physical outcomes, and the subsequent user-requested camera-mount rollback. The physical C episode stops and returns after 24 actions; S stops after 13 actions with an unsafe-anchor method failure and does not return. Initial maps and observations differ; neither outcome establishes a semantic causal advantage or physical reconstruction accuracy. The proposed 900 s deadline was rejected before motion and the actual predeclared revision uses 600 s for both arms. Raw RGB-D, full decision logs, failure rings, and images remain local; summaries and hashes alone are not full sensor replay.

The new focused article includes its required LaTeX inputs and figure PDFs. The graduation thesis contains editable Markdown and build scripts; existing unchanged thesis `assets/`, `figures/` and `math_assets/` are reused from the previously published `docs/thesis/graduation_integrated_20261104_two_room/` tree and must be placed at the same relative paths before a standalone rebuild. New Q1 data and plotting source are in `integrations/rapid_evidence_20261108/semantic/`; new pair results are in `integrations/rapid_evidence_20261108/physical/`. The current camera launch is restored to the earlier mount; the calibrated trial remains an actual historical experiment and its prior source is preserved in the rollback record.
