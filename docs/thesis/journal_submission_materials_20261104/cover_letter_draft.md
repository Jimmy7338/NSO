To the Editors, *Journal of Intelligent & Robotic Systems*

Proposed manuscript: “Correctable Category Priors for Budget-Constrained Active Observation”

Dear Editors,

Please consider this manuscript for publication as an original research article. It examines when a category cue changes the choice of an observation route under a shared sensing and return budget, and how acquired geometry can correct that cue. The policy uses public configuration hypotheses for planning while reconstructing surfaces only from measured depth.

A matched geometric control retains the planner, diagnostic opportunities, navigation graph and fusion backend. In controlled simulations, four reference configuration pairs yield two improvements and two ties, with a 6.16% mean increase in coverage–surface quality at unchanged coverage. Four changed-background pairs yield one improvement and three ties, with a 2.98% mean increase. The manuscript retains low-budget failures, a larger-budget reversal and neutral or adverse multi-instance results. These results describe conditional observation benefits, rather than general superiority.

Separate unknown-space exploration diagnostics distinguish the original policy from an explicitly revised deployment variant. A Q0-derived two-room case links a strict category-dependent ranking reversal at the same observed state to subsequent differences in surface observation. Its category channel uses synthetic markers. A subsequent fixed-weight geometric control reproduces that entire trajectory, so this case does not isolate an advantage specific to category information. A bounded robot episode demonstrates the deployment variant's sensing–navigation–return loop with a natural-image semantic frontend; it does not establish comparative physical semantic benefit or reconstruction accuracy. The manuscript keeps these evidence scopes separate.

The study addresses robotic observation planning and the use of category information before geometric configuration is fully resolved. Reproduction materials include code, frozen configurations, complete comparison records, failures, editable figures and independent replay checks.
