\section{Two-template gain within unknown-space exploration}
\label{sec:dual-template-unknown}

This declared composition retains the observed-state v10\_3\_1 unknown-coverage and measured-quality score $J_t(r)$, candidate generator, execution primitives, and stopping/return rules. It replaces the entire analytic AABB prior with the V3 box/shelf module's expected visible area:
\begin{equation}
U_t^m(r)=J_t(r)+\sum_i\max_{v\in r^+} g_{t,i}^m(v),\qquad m\in\{G,S\}.
\end{equation}
Here $r^+$ contains committed outbound successors only, and $g$ is the inherited area-weighted visibility with camera field of view, measured-TSDF occlusion, and per-hypothesis self-occlusion. The pointwise maximum avoids repeated reward. Its coefficient is one, retaining the replaced prior's area convention rather than the V3 controller's different normalization. V3 candidate proposals are not used. Shared observed-instance support partitions measured samples, including an unassigned partition, before geometric grouping. Thus this is a new variant, separate from the V35 supplied-graph study and either original controller.

Both G and S fit the same two geometric hypotheses. Their initial shelf weights are respectively $0.5$ and $0.1+0.8f_{\rm open}$, where $f_{\rm open}$ is the observed open-category vote fraction; sensor labels 2/open and 3/closed are translated to the V3 dictionary, 3/shelf and 2/box. The update adds $(e_{\rm box}-e_{\rm shelf})/(2\times0.06^2)$ to the prior log odds, clips to $[-6,6]$, and applies the logistic function. The $e$ values are mean squared measured-point distances, truncated at 0.15~m. This model-discrepancy pseudo-posterior is rebuilt from the prior and current measurements, without recursively reusing the previous posterior. It is not calibrated uncertainty or a trained shape network. Historical depth removes reward samples already measured within 0.10~m or contradicted by measured free space. It does not modify the posterior through free-space evidence or change the self-occlusion meshes: a contradicted backboard can remain an occluder.

\paragraph{Fixed protocol.}
The two controlled layouts swap original, unscaled ReplicaCAD shelf 31 and complete cabinet 118 in a synthetic $10\times8$~m branching floorplan. Front, rear, and side views are geometrically reachable. These are two related permutations of one authored scene family, not natural-distribution samples. The identical first-frame initialization leaves 57.39\% of 1,366 reachable cells unknown. Sensors use $120\times90$ RGB-D, $90^{\circ}$ horizontal field of view, 8~m range, 1.2~m camera height, zero depth noise, and exact pose. Labels and instance support are ideal current first-hit observations, not RGB-detector predictions. The actor receives no reference map, source bounds, or hidden mesh. All arms share a 0.2~m-radius robot, mesh-based swept-footprint/floor checks, 0.2~m forward actions, $90^{\circ}$ turns, and a 256-paid-action cap including return actions. The 1.98~m shelf without a backboard and 0.78~m cabinet do not match the inherited 0.9--1.5~m template-height range or optional shelf backboard; these mismatches are retained.

C uses coverage only; J retains coverage and measured quality without a template prior. G adds neutral-prior templates; S adds category-prior templates. $S_{\rm no\ fit}$ disables the geometric-fit log-ratio. $S_{\rm no\ history}$ jointly disables already-measured reward consumption and free-space rejection, while retaining measured mapping. Each arm runs once in each layout. TSDF fusion uses 6~cm voxels and 12~cm truncation. Surface evaluation uses the same fixed visible source-mesh reference within each layout and 12,000 predicted-surface samples, without alignment or predicted-template insertion.

\paragraph{Results and attribution.}
All twelve runs attain 100\% correct-free coverage of the start-reachable reference, with zero simulated collisions and a return to the initial pose. C reaches full coverage sooner and stops after 130 actions; the other arms exhaust 256. G and S have identical complete trajectories and TSDF meshes in both layouts, as does $S_{\rm no\ fit}$ relative to S. Category-dependent scores are nonzero in 5/25 and 4/26 S planning states, but do not change the G-selected candidate. Against J at the same action budget, S reduces whole-scene F1 by 0.36 percentage points and travels 1.6~m farther in the left-shelf layout; the right-shelf layout ties. Removing history changes both trajectories and shifts F1 by $+0.24$ and $-0.06$ percentage points. It also activates the inherited 3,500-point weighted thinning in 28/14 planning states (left/right), versus none in the other arms; this comparison therefore includes a sampling effect. Higher furniture completeness relative to C accompanies 126 additional actions and establishes neither semantic nor efficiency benefit. The comparison demonstrates complete exploration by this composition under controlled sensing; it does not validate a benefit from category weighting, fit correction, or history rejection, or establish detector-level or physical performance. The earlier natural-apartment negative results remain separate.
