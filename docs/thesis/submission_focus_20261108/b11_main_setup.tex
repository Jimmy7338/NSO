\subsection{Forecast Selection and Complete Exploration in New Layouts}
We freeze four new layouts with two room-connectivity topologies, a four-room chain and a four-room cycle, and vary their dimensions, doorway positions, and furniture arrangements. The layouts reuse the development shelf and cabinet assets, so they test new spatial arrangements rather than unseen CAD categories. Sensing retains exact poses, ideal visible instance/category labels, noiseless opaque RGB-D, and the measured-only TSDF. At an identical acquired state, the visibility and acquisition forecasts share the same observation-fitted geometry, full geometric and structural posterior, original $0.22\,$m quadrature, category prior, unknown-coverage and measured-surface scores, and execution rules. Only the predicted template-area term changes. The acquisition forecast independently fuses all acquired frames with hypothetical future depth; predicted geometry never enters the actual map.

Each layout supplies two scripted acquisition checkpoints. At each state, the largest original-candidate group in an outbound-action cost band of width three is chosen before either completion forecast or any future outcome is evaluated. Three routes contain both category-prior forecasts' selections from this cost-matched group, with remaining slots filled by candidate identifier. All four forecast/prior combinations are re-ranked on this same set. The neutral and category-conditioned interventions retain the same fitted geometry, visible-label instance associations, and frontend; only their structural class-prior weights differ. Two checkpoints initially lacking three cost-close candidates are advanced to the earliest cost-feasible state along their frozen scripts under a prospectively declared amendment; all intervening failures and the other six states are retained. Each of the twenty-four branches is executed once from an identical prefix copy. Its measured result is shared by both forecasts. Fixed independently sampled source points yield gross-new, lost, and net object surface at $5\,$cm. We report chosen gross area, its gap to the best of three, MAE, unchanged decisions, failures, and recorded time; reference geometry is reserved for evaluation.

After the first twelve branches are tabulated and the remaining twelve are evaluated, each forecast is run from a cold start in every layout with the category prior. These eight complete episodes use the inherited full candidate generator without the short diagnostic's cost band, the same initial packet within each layout, and $256$ paid actions including return. Metrics are first actions to $95\%$ and $100\%$ correct-free start-reachable coverage, object recall, whole-scene F1, actual path length, collisions, and return. Missing thresholds remain censored at executed actions. The four layouts are comparison units; their eight checkpoints, twenty-four candidate branches, and eight complete episodes do not constitute additional independent environments. All failures and negative outcomes remain included. The supplement gives the complete preparation amendment, source hashes, per-state results, and metric definitions.

The shared simulator uses a 0.2\,m circular radius, $120\times90$ RGB-D at $90^{\circ}$ field of view, an 8\,m range, and 180 planar scan beams. The measured CPU TSDF uses 6\,cm voxels and 12\,cm truncation. Exact virtual poses and ideal visible labels isolate planning effects from localization and detector errors.
