# Figure captions

**Paired action-96 net shelf area.** Each row is one independent room–shelf unit. The two symbols are the structure-guided minus direct-inspection area difference with the same front unobstructed or partly screened. Net area is the change in shelf reference area represented by the measured mesh relative to the common cold map within each placement. The vertical line marks equal area. Missing symbols indicate that the corresponding comparison could not be measured.

**Mechanism example, shelf_01_screen.** The registered case selection scans screened placements first and favors the earliest verified pair with an executed divergence and return. The common camera frame and the complete executed traces locate the fork. Dotted lines are the independently nominated outbound plans. Dark lines show their realized motion before arrival or interruption, and pale lines show neutral continuation. Squares locate the nominated goals and crosses mark an interruption. Both surface panels use the same oblique projection of fixed reference samples registered to the common camera axes. Colored points at action 96 indicate samples that were unrepresented at the fork and measured by only S or D. The final panel displays samples that retain that same exclusive status at actual return. The grey reference is evaluator-only geometry.
