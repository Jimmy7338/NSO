## Reuse decisions

- Native sensor renderer, fixed footprint and return guard, RGB-D TSDF, deterministic visibility score, local candidate generation and reference triangle evaluator are reused. The circulation path and geometry of known Chain/Cycle assets are development or regression only.
- The regional candidates use the original oriented reachability graph and return reservation. The structural ranking is the experimental global switch. The local comparison preserves the original neutral and category-conditioned support and the same geometric auxiliary weights.
- Learned long-horizon or feasibility models, source-only room graphs and the expensive fusion-aware forecast are historical or diagnostic. Study code, state and evidence are stored under this directory. The thesis Word source remains an independent artifact.

## Completed development stage

The native regression and its separately evaluated region-enabled counterpart matched the same cold packet. Both finished and returned at action 192. The switch generated two dispatched new regional targets and re-ranked two native alternatives. First-new-room time remained 26 actions. Final coverage was 100% for both, surface F1 fell by 2.67 percentage points, and repeated-edge distance increased by 0.8 m.
