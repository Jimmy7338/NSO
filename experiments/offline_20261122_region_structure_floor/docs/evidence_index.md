## Reuse decisions

- Native sensor renderer, fixed footprint and return guard, RGB-D TSDF, deterministic visibility score, local candidate generation and reference triangle evaluator are reused. The circulation path and geometry of known Chain/Cycle assets are development or regression only.
- The regional candidates use the original oriented reachability graph and return reservation. The structural ranking is the experimental global switch. The local comparison preserves the original neutral and category-conditioned support and the same geometric auxiliary weights.
- Learned long-horizon or feasibility models, source-only room graphs and the expensive fusion-aware forecast are historical or diagnostic. Study code, state and evidence are stored under this directory. The thesis Word source remains an independent artifact.

## Completed development stage

The native regression and its separately evaluated region-enabled counterpart matched the same cold packet. Both finished and returned at action 192. The switch generated two dispatched new regional targets and re-ranked two native alternatives. First-new-room time remained 26 actions. Final coverage was 100% for both, surface F1 fell by 2.67 percentage points, and repeated-edge distance increased by 0.8 m. ## Earlier four-layout comparison and independent calibration

The B11 follow-up pairs four new neutral-prior runs with the original four category-prior visibility runs, using the same cold packets and 256-action cap. Three paired runs reproduce the exact trajectory and mesh. The remaining category-prior run reduces area-weighted object recall by 3.79 percentage points, with unchanged 95% and 100% coverage clocks. Four strict nearest-frontier runs use the same renderer and guard, reach 95% coverage in all four layouts and reach full coverage in three. The four earlier stationary frontier runs are retained as a candidate-generation failure case. These fixed-branch observations quantify the error of the predictor, with exact simulated pose and ideal visible labels. The four-layout follow-up and its calibration have a different budget, scene set and experimental role from the current 192-action floor matrix. ## Estimated pose and archived robot evidence

Two geometry-only trajectories in the ordinary held-out floor use the vehicle's CSM implementation in an isolated scan replay. Each replays 36 actions and matches all 37 native boundary-scan digests. ## Completed held-out floor matrix

All 20 episodes supplied an evaluated endpoint and returned without simulated collisions under the common 192-action cap. Relative to original G, the combined method changed mean terminal J by +0.06 percentage point. Relative to strict nearest frontier it changed J by −1.15 points. The regional factor reduced repeated travel by 1.48 m on average and delayed the first physical entry to a new room by 1.75 actions. The category factor changed J by +0.08 points with the original global strategy and +0.93 points with the regional strategy. These are building-level descriptive effects. ## Same-goal local structure versus direct inspection

The local protocol covered two parent layouts with a visible and a screened placement in each. Fourteen of 16 registered runs have complete verified ledgers, with 11 returns and no simulated collisions in the verified runs. The two missing terminal records are retained Open3D failures in the east screened placement. On the two visible placements, category-conditioned S produced 0.103 and 0.196 m² less net shelf area than direct inspection at action 96. On the west screened placement, a registered earlier checkpoint at action 48 favored S by 0.488 m². Neutral G, correct-category S and swapped-category X stopped at action 61 because the return anchor became disconnected in the observed map. Their action-96 outcomes are missing.
