# Development pair

We compared the original neutral global selector and the region-aware selector from the same cold frame in a known four-room cyclic floor. Both used the frozen, tuned geometric local scorer. The regional switch changed the first dispatched macro target, which its arm reached at paid action 29 compared with action 38 for the original selector's target. Their first executed command differed at action 28. Both entered a new room at action 26. With the region layer, the third and fourth rooms were reached at actions 77 and 105, compared with 84 and 125, while the 95% true-free coverage clock changed from 93 to 92. Repeated-edge travel increased from 5.2 to 6.0 m. Both arms returned after 192 charged actions with 100% reachable-free coverage and zero simulated collisions. Whole-scene F1 at 5 cm fell from 97.49% to 94.82%, with surface recall changing from 95.27% to 90.29%. Joint map quality J fell by 2.67 percentage points. This single known development floor verifies end-to-end regional dispatch and reveals a trade-off between earlier access to later rooms and final surface reconstruction.
