# Matched same-goal inspection

We fixed four placements in two synthetic parent layouts with an accessible side-view position, and compared the exposed condition with an opaque screen. The four policies shared the semantic observation input, global goal and full legal route pool at the choice point. They then collected and fused depth independently. The direct-inspection baseline used the same ideal visible category labels and ranked routes by the angular undercoverage, range and incidence of previously measured fixture surfels. The category-conditioned scorer predicted surface visibility using a structural posterior. 14/16 episodes are verified and 2/4 placements yield computable focal contrasts. The net newly represented shelf area for category-conditioned planning minus direct inspection at action 96 and at the actual terminal was east_visible -0.1031/-0.0458 m², west_visible -0.1960/-0.1287 m². The selected branch reached its goal in 7 episodes. Across verified episodes, 11 returned and 0 simulated collisions were recorded. All charged turns and return actions counted against the 192-action budget. Placements within each parent were paired controls.
