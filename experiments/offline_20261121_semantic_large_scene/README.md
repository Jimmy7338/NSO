# Facilities and unknown multi-room reconstruction

The primary endpoint is the product of correctly mapped start-reachable free space and whole-scene F1 at 5 cm. The endpoint uses the actual recorded stop, with return actions counted against the common cap. The two furniture placements within a parent share the same reachability, initially seen geometry and source area. They provide paired evidence for that parent.

- `PROTOCOL.json` freezes the common endpoint and the mechanism gate.
- `e1` contains the exact historical 198-action restoration and the two candidate interventions, source digests, trace manifests and independent surface evaluation.
- `mechanism/scenes` contains the four controlled placements, visible-support and reachability checks, reference generator and frozen evaluator inputs.
- `mechanism/runner` contains the blind selection hook, all execution receipts and the supplementary read-only optical audit on native candidate poses.
- `evaluation/historical_scope.json` records which previous results can serve as historical context. The previously examined smaller layouts remain outside any new confirmation batch.
- `evaluation/reproduce.sh` recomputes endpoints, checks the matched decision, applies the declared gate and generates the figure from the terminal artifacts. The raw-evidence index records hashes and local locations where the full geometry and arrays are retained.

The score uses only acquired observations and visible ideal class and instance evidence. Fixed source meshes and reachable-space masks are used for simulation geometry and independent evaluation. Route scope and early optical evidence are evaluated separately from the final reconstructed surface. Optical surface bins count distinct first-hit cells on the open shelf, and do not represent measured mesh area.
