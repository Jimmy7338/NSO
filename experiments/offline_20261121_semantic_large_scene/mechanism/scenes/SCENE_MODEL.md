# Controlled facility pair

The warehouse shell is 11.0 × 12.4 m. The gallery shell is 12.2 × 14.2 m with an additional annex accessed through a single doorway after the two lanes merge. In each parent, a central opaque divider separates two traversable lanes between the launch court and a shared initially unmapped space. The annex belongs to both alternatives. The two side swaps in each parent use the same architecture, external asset envelopes, plinths, side panels, scanner height, camera geometry and motion grid. The camera first sees matching south side panels and plinths. At the aperture, the open shelving exposes shelf and divider interiors while the cabinet presents a door. Layout labels describe ideal simulator-provided visible category support. Neither early RGB appearance nor the silhouette encodes which class is on which side.

The scene protocol is defined in `mechanism_world.py`. `prepare.py` validates physical/circular-sweep reachability, measured initial map parity, source triangle area, exact first-view sensor parity and symmetric routes that end at the same initially unknown cell. Its camera first-hit counts verify that only the expected lane sees shelf interiors before it reaches the common merger row. Counts sum actual first-hit pixels across views and do not measure unique surface area. The legal nominal paths check the geometry and do not enter candidate generation or execute as policies. Candidate coverage and ranking must be evaluated from the real online records, including cases with no qualifying route or no trigger.

The evaluation-only lane label is the first northbound crossing of the declared row outside the central ±0.8 m band. The `*_left` and `*_right` variants fix the expected informative side. The route and potential later actual crossing are separate events. A route which has no such crossing is left unclassified. This label and the canonical probe paths do not enter the actor. The scene reference is frozen using the same path-independent first-hit lattice and 5 cm mesh metric from B11. The legacy keys `open31`/`closed118` identify the two categories in the reference only. These are procedural fixtures with deliberately aligned views and noiseless depth/exact pose, and not an independently sampled dataset of buildings or natural detectors.
