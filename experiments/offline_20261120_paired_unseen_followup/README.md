# NSO matched exploration and fitted-gain follow-up

The experiment extends the frozen B11 study in `experiments/offline_20261120_paired_unseen`. The four historical S visibility episodes supply matched controls. Four new G visibility episodes change only which structural-prior score determines the target. The observed instance frontend and visibility model are shared. The new nearest-frontier episodes use independent frontier candidates and share the original B11 sensors, map updates and action guard. The original frontier implementation adds the current position even outside the frontier. Its four runs are retained. A separately declared four-run variant removes that unconditional addition and otherwise retains the candidate selection rule and executor.

The calibration study uses eight additional constructed layouts, each with unique furniture geometry and three executed branches. Four layouts set coefficients and the other four evaluate them. The B11 layouts supply neither calibration nor holdout observations. The learned correction scales the acquisition forecast's modeled gross-new area according to distance from the corresponding acquired instance support. Short branches evaluate prediction and selected-route consequences under ideal simulated sensing. They do not constitute a new closed-loop method comparison.

`evaluation/results/layouts.csv` holds the common end metrics. The archived evaluation includes per-layout paired comparisons, censored thresholds, intervention counts, source verification and all terminal/failed receipts. The archived calibration records include the declared split, frozen coefficients, per-route measurements and the held-out aggregates. The archive index lists each member's SHA-256. Raw arrays, TSDF meshes and source geometry retained locally are listed separately with their sizes and hashes.
