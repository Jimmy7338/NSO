TWO_ROOM_01 is one hand-designed Q0-derived related scene with a divider and doorway, not an independent population sample. The two frozen G/S 96-action runs are a separate diagnostic and are not included in the published 24-trajectory main matrix. Preflight preceded policy outcomes; failed initial blocked geometry is retained. The portable harness only changes paths and its preflight runner hash; original scripts, frozen preflight, and all execution records are retained. `MANIFEST.json` records hashes. Simulator truth is used for evaluation, not policy observations; no robot or ROS is used.

Both runs crossed the doorway at action 19, returned to the anchor after 96 actions with zero simulated collisions, and finished at 99.01% coverage. G/S first reached 99% at actions 58/56; final simulated visible surface was 123.965/126.577 m². Paths and surface first diverged at action 31, when both had 98.31% coverage. These are single deterministic runs in one related scene and do not establish generalization, physical benefit or reconstruction accuracy. Detailed metrics and full trajectories are under `frozen_execution`.

# Fixed two-room mechanism scene

`TWO_ROOM_01` is one hand-designed, Q0-derived controlled test, not a random, representative, or independent scene sample. It retains Q0 shelf-west physical assets, marker semantics, noise seed, sensor and motion configuration, simulator and joint policy implementation. It adds a 0.2 m thick wall at y=3.6 m with a physical doorway x=3.0–4.6 m. This creates two rooms joined through one opening; sealing the divider separates the starting component from all northern free components. Truth-based occupancy and mesh are used only by simulator and evaluator; policy input remains measured RGB-D, lidar, discrete exact odometry.

Preflight tested only construction, connectivity, initial camera/lidar and mapping, and a zero-cost initial return plan. It performed no policy run and did not inspect trajectory outcomes. Initial cell is safe after the 0.2 m circular guard; 426 northern and 576 southern reachable center cells exist; first-frame coverage is 60.12%. This does not prove that the joint planner can discover or pass the doorway. Initial construction placed the door at x=5.2–6.8; preflight found the inflated object/door blocked almost all the north side (six center cells). The retained `geometry_preflight_initial_blocked.json` records that failed geometry; the current door was relocated before policy results existed.

Frozen prospective condition is one G and one S run at total 96 paid actions, fresh identical first packet and anchor, same observed guard and full-pose return contract as joint v10_3_1 runner, and no other method parameter changed. `preflight.json` freezes source, world, runner, parameters and initial packet hashes. `runner.py` is a local adaptation of published deployed-policy matrix runner; it checks those hashes and initial packet before running. It has only passed Python compilation and scene/sensor preflight: policy execution is not yet tested. It creates `two_room_joint` there. The runner records failures rather than hiding them. No robot or ROS is used.

No results exist yet, and a single fixed Q0-derived construction cannot establish generalization or physical semantic benefit.
