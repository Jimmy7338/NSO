# Comparison audit

The two matrices use the same frozen research source SHA-256 map, Q0 generator (outer seed 951 and its deterministic sensor seed), Q0 `shelf_west`/`shelf_east`, initial sensor packet and anchor (verified across all eight runs per arrangement), budgets 48/96, zero paid prefix, simulated RGB-D and scan, exact simulated odometry, 0.2 m grid and circular robot radius 0.2 m. They use the same evaluator, reference surface sampling seed 2026, metrics, score-mode parameters, and G/S weights. `exploration_joint_v1` is a modified policy on the v10.3.1 core, not original v10.3.1; its three changes are documented in the deployed protocol/probe and its physical range, extent, instance and natural-semantic adapters were disabled. These are two arrangements of one Q0 context, not two independent scenes. The two matrix runs occurred separately; source and initial packet matching do not constitute a hardware test.

`snapshot_metrics(..., (.05,.10))` passes thresholds in metres; `reconstruction_metrics.py` evaluates distances with `<= threshold` and formats `round(threshold*100)` as a two-digit centimetre tag. Thus `f1_05cm` means F1 at **5 cm = 0.05 m**, not 0.5 cm. Path length here counts 0.2 m for each forward action; rotations have no translation. Truth-visible surface is a reference-mesh visibility measure, not measured real-world reconstruction accuracy.

Within each policy, action sequences are identical between G and S for west48, east48 and east96; both policies first differ on west96 at action 31. For deployed west96, planning call 2 after action 21 has identical recorded planning inputs (including feedback versions, map version and planning camera pose hash), remaining budget 75, and the same candidate list. G selects pose [5,29,2] (candidate 8), while S selects [5,10,2] (candidate 7). Candidate 8 vs 7: G scores 11.0078 vs 9.9987; S scores 9.0589 vs 12.1566. The recorded common proxies are 8.2092 vs 6.8780, while S prior potentials are 0.8497 vs 5.2786. At this shared decision, switching the scoring mode changes the candidate ranking and selected target under the recorded decomposition. This establishes the cause of that selection difference in this decision; it does not establish how much of the final outcome difference is attributable to that selection, or whether the effect generalizes.

The trajectories share actions through step 30; at step 31 G turns right and S turns left. Newly truth-visible surface is equal at step 30 (46.4565 m²), G leads at step 60 (98.3907 vs 71.8195 m²), and S leads at step 80 (116.2320 vs 105.1402 m²) and at step 96 (118.5212 vs 109.5097 m²). The final 9.0115 m² difference is associated with the later paths and observation history, not a validated general mechanism. The same comparison must retain original v10.3.1's west96 negative S outcome, as well as all other ties. No independent seeds or uncertainty intervals are available.
